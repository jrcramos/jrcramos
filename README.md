## 💡 About Me  

Born on a farm in Cape Verde, raised with curiosity and care, and trained across Portugal and Germany, I bring a **systems-thinking approach to life** — whether I'm growing vegetables, building neural networks, or modeling virus dynamics inside living cells.

As a bioprocess engineer and computational biologist, my work sits at the **intersection of biology, data science, and systems optimization**. My research focuses on:
- **Hybrid deep modeling** of bioprocesses combining mechanistic knowledge with deep neural networks
- **In silico media/feed design** using hybrid semi-parametric modeling (contributing to >50% gains in mammalian cell productivity)
- **Mechanistic dynamical models** of cell growth, central carbon metabolism, and virus production
- Statistical modeling, machine learning, and dynamic systems design

> *"Engineering is not just about precision — it's also about care, consciousness, and designing for what truly matters."*

**Outside the lab:** I am a digital "nerd" building automation tools leveraging math, programing and AI,  such as making websites, automation bots, and quantitative finantial models. To disconnect, I  read, focus on distance running, games, and working on our mini farm tending to the garden, chickens, and goats. Music is the constant backdrop to both my work and life — even while working! 🎵
